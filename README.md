## 💼 Featured Projects

### 🏥 GAHAR Healthcare Accreditation Platform

A healthcare accreditation software platform designed to help hospitals and healthcare facilities in Egypt organize their accreditation journey.

**Key Features**

- Applicability assessment and standards scope identification.
- Self-assessment and gap analysis.
- Evidence and document management.
- AI-assisted accreditation guidance.
- Document generation and review workflows.
- Compliance monitoring and reporting.
- Multi-tenant architecture and role-based access control.

**Technologies:** Flutter, Node.js, Express.js, PostgreSQL, Supabase.

---

### 🏭 Factory ERP System

A factory management system designed to organize manufacturing operations and business processes in one application.

**Key Features**

- Inventory and materials management.
- Employee and attendance management.
- Finance and accounting.
- Production and order management.
- Business dashboards and analytics.
- Role-based access and permissions.
- Offline support and data synchronization.

**Technologies:** Flutter, React, TypeScript, IndexedDB, and related backend technologies.
